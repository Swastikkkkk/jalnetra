# JalNetra live demo script (3 minutes)

1. Open the site. Home shows the LIVE status of the Gandak basin right now (GloFAS + Open-Meteo, real).
   Say: "Right now JalNetra is monitoring the Gandak basin."
2. Click **Run live demo simulation**. The badge says LIVE DEMO SIMULATION: the September 2026 flood replayed as if it were happening now; the model only sees data up to the simulated clock.
3. Press **8 s** (faster model runs) or **+3 h now**. Watch the river forecast turn "Rising rapidly", the striped predicted flood expand, and "Villages at risk" climb. Slide NOW / +6H / +24H.
4. The alert queue fills with HIGH and CRITICAL villages. Click one: risk, probability, time to impact, why (factor bars + model terms) and the evacuation route that avoids likely water and never crosses the river.
5. **Review and approve alert**: the "Why this alert was created" box, recipients (demo phone), Hindi message. **Approve alert and call**. The incident is created, approved, the Hindi AI call goes out, status becomes SARPANCH CONTACTED.
6. On the phone ask "हमें कहाँ जाना चाहिए?" The agent answers with the shelter and route from this prediction.
7. Click **2026 validation**: left = JalNetra forecast issued 24 h before the radar pass, right = what Sentinel-1 saw. Predicted / observed / correctly identified, with the method.
   Say: "We use the 2026 event to check whether the predictions line up with what actually happened."
8. End: DETECT, PREDICT, WARN, EVACUATE, VERIFY.
